From your playing to a score in under a minute.

1

Record or upload

2

What you get

Made for practising and learning your own playing.

Guitarist-like tab

Strings and frets chosen the way a player would, not just the lowest fret.

Chords and strums

Chord names above the music, strummed chords kept together, with their direction.

Synced playback

Hear your own recording with the score following along, or a guitar or piano sound.

An editor

Change string, fret, pitch or length, add or remove notes, and go back to any version.

Export

PDF to print, Guitar Pro for tab apps, MusicXML for notation apps, MIDI for your DAW.

Private by design

Your transcriptions are yours alone, and your recording is deleted from our servers once it’s transcribed.

Questions

 

What can it transcribe?

One instrument at a time: solo guitar (strummed chords, riffs, lead, fingerpicking) or solo piano. Full band recordings come later.

Is it accurate?

It gets most notes right on clear recordings, but transcription is automatic and can make mistakes. The editor is there to fix them quickly.

Does it cost anything?

Riffscribe is free, with a monthly number of transcriptions.

What happens to my recordings?

They’re used only to make your transcription and are deleted from our servers right after. Your transcriptions are private: nothing is published or shared. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. See the privacy policy.

When is the iPhone version coming?

After the Android launch. The page will link to the App Store as soon as it’s there.
