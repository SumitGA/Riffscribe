Delete your Riffscribe account

Riffscribe · Sumit Gautam

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Riffscribe and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email sumitga.playstore@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Riffscribe account”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • All your transcriptions, their versions and edits, and their files.
  • Your notification settings, and the recordings the app kept on your phone (when deleted in the app).

Nothing is kept afterwards. Database backups containing your account expire within 14 days.
