Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

This policy explains what Riffscribe, an app by Sumit Gautam, collects, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: sumitga.playstore@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and profile picture from Google or your email sign-in, held by our sign-in provider Clerk. Our servers store only your account ID and when you accepted the terms.
  • Recordings you upload: used only to make your transcription, then deleted from our servers. The app keeps a copy on your phone so you can play it with the score.
  • Transcriptions: the sheet music, tab, MIDI and your edits, kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Usage: how many transcriptions you made this month (for the free limit), and the status, timing and any errors of each transcription.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, an address our servers use to tell your phone when a score is ready.
  • Technical logs: server logs with account and transcription IDs and error messages, used to keep the service running, kept for a limited time.

The app uses the microphone only while you record. We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising ID. There are no ads and no third-party analytics, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

To provide the service: sign-in, transcription, storing your scores, the free monthly limit, notifications, fixing problems and keeping the service secure.

Who processes it

  • Clerk — sign-in and account details.
  • Cloudflare — secure network access to our servers, and file storage (R2).
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Expo — delivering notifications and app updates.
  • Our own servers in Australia — transcription and the database.

Some of these providers may process data outside Australia.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded audio: deleted from our servers once processed.
  • Transcriptions: until you delete them or your account.
  • When you delete your account, everything is deleted straight away; database backups containing it expire within 14 days.

Your choices

  • Delete a transcription, or your whole account (Account → Delete account, or see this page).
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in your phone’s settings.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or for a correction, by emailing sumitga.playstore@gmail.com.

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Files are private to your account and reached only through short-lived links.

Children

Riffscribe is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page when this policy changes, with the date above.
