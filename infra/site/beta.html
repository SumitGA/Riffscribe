Join the beta

Riffscribe for Android is in a free private beta. Play guitar or piano into your phone and get the tab and sheet music. It takes two taps to join.

1Join the testers’ group

Open the link and tap Join group. You won’t get emails from other testers.

Join the group

2Become a tester

Open the link and tap Become a tester, then download it on Google Play.

Become a tester

3Try it

Open Riffscribe, sign in, and record a few seconds of guitar or piano. Then play it back, fix a note, or export it.

Use the same Google account as on your phone for both steps, or Google Play won’t show you the app. If the link says the app isn’t available yet, wait a minute after joining the group and try again.

Please keep the app installed for two weeks. Google only lets new apps launch after a two-week test, and every tester counts. Tell us what works and what doesn’t at sumitga.playstore@gmail.com.

On iPhone? The iPhone version comes after the Android launch.
